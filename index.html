Feature 01 · Romantic Comedy

The Proposal

A summer box-office triumph powered by two decades of real friendship.

Released
June 19, 2009 (US)
Director

  1. 01

    Box Office Success

    Released in the United States on June 19, 2009, the movie became a massive commercial hit, grossing over $317 million worldwide and earning the title of the highest-grossing romantic comedy of 2009.

  2. 02

    Stellar Chemistry & Friendship

    Sandra Bullock and Ryan Reynolds had been close friends for over 20 years before filming, which is why their on-screen chemistry and comedic timing feel so natural and effortless.

  3. 03

    Filming Location

    Although the story is set in Sitka, Alaska, the movie was actually filmed in the picturesque towns of Rockport and Gloucester in Massachusetts, USA.

  4. 04

    Written Specially for Julia Roberts

    The screenplay was originally written with Julia Roberts in mind for the lead role of Margaret Tate, though Sandra Bullock ultimately took the part and delivered an iconic performance.

Feature 02 · Drama

My Sister’s Keeper

An emotional story of family, love and the choices that test them.

My Sister’s Keeper is an American drama film released on June 26, 2009. It was directed by Nick Cassavetes and is based on the 2004 novel by Jodi Picoult.

The main actors are Cameron Diaz, Abigail Breslin, Sofia Vassilieva, Alec Baldwin and Jason Patric.

The movie was filmed mainly in Los Angeles, California, USA. It was produced by New Line Cinema and other companies.

The film had a budget of $30 million and earned around $95.7 million worldwide. Its running time is 109 minutes.

The movie did not receive any Oscar nominations, but it won a 2009 Teen Choice Award. Abigail Breslin and Sofia Vassilieva also won Young Artist Awards in 2010.

Interesting Facts

  1. The movie has a different ending from the original book.

  2. Cameron Diaz played a serious role, unlike many of her usual comedy films.

Loneliness, friendship and trust in a cold, melancholic 1980s winter.

The film “Let Me In” is a psychological horror-drama that explores loneliness and the need for acceptance, blending the element of vampirism into a cold, melancholic 1980s setting.

Feature 04 · Survival Drama · Based on a True Story

127Hours

One climber, one canyon in Utah, and a story of survival that earned six Academy Award nominations.

2010

Small budget, big return

Worldwide box office≈ $60.7M

6

Academy Award nominations in 2011, including Best Picture, Best Actor and Best Adapted Screenplay.

  1. 01

    Box Office Success

    Released in 2010, the movie earned approximately $60.7 million worldwide, despite having a production budget of only $18 million.

  2. 02

    Based on a True Story

    The film tells the real-life story of Aron Ralston, a mountain climber who became trapped in Bluejohn Canyon, Utah, in 2003. His incredible experience inspired the movie.

  3. 03

    Outstanding Performance

    James Franco played Aron Ralston and received an Academy Award nomination for Best Actor in 2011.

  4. 04

    Filming Location

    Unlike many films that recreate locations in studios, 127 Hours was filmed in Utah, USA, including the actual canyon where Aron Ralston’s accident took place.

  5. 05

    Award Recognition

    The film received six Academy Award nominations in 2011, including Best Picture, Best Actor and Best Adapted Screenplay.

  6. 06

    A Personal Connection

    The real Aron Ralston was involved in the production and helped James Franco understand his experiences so that he could portray the character more realistically.

Feature 05 · Historical Drama

The King’s Speech

An inspiring film about friendship, overcoming personal challenges, and finding one’s voice.

The King’s Speech is a British historical drama film released in late 2010 and early 2011. It was directed by Tom Hooper and written by David Seidler.

The main actors are Colin Firth, Geoffrey Rush, Helena Bonham Carter, Guy Pearce, and Michael Gambon.

The movie was filmed mainly in London and historic locations across England. It was produced by See-Saw Films and Bedlam Productions.

The film had a budget of $15 million and earned around $424 million worldwide. Its running time is 118 minutes.

The movie was a massive critical success, winning four Academy Awards in 2011: Best Picture, Best Director, Best Original Screenplay, and Best Actor for Colin Firth.

The Evidence

  1. Exhibit A

    Box Office Success

    Released on December 25, 2009, the movie became a commercial success, earning over $524 million worldwide and becoming one of the most successful detective films of its time.

  2. Exhibit B

    Robert Downey Jr.’s Transformation

    Robert Downey Jr. completely embraced the role of Sherlock Holmes, bringing a unique combination of intelligence, humour and eccentric behaviour to the famous detective.

  3. Exhibit C

    Filming Location

    Although the story takes place in Victorian London in 1891, much of the movie was filmed in London and other locations across England, using detailed historical sets to recreate the atmosphere of the 19th century.

  4. Exhibit D

    Unique Detective Style

    Unlike the traditional image of Sherlock Holmes, this version portrays him as an energetic, unconventional detective who uses scientific experiments, careful observations and incredible fighting skills to solve mysteries.

  5. Exhibit E

    Additional Filming Locations

    Although the story is set in Victorian London, filming also took place in Manchester, Liverpool and other locations across England.

Section 07 · Language Lab

Cinema Vocabulary

Explore the language of film genres.

  1. 01

    Action and Adventure Films

    ENFilms featuring exciting action, dangerous situations, physical challenges, and adventurous journeys.

    SQFilma aksion dhe aventurë – filma që përmbajnë skena emocionuese, situata të rrezikshme, sfida fizike dhe udhëtime aventureske.

    In this edition: 127 Hours, Sherlock Holmes

  2. 02

    Animations

    ENFilms created using drawings, computer-generated images, or other animation techniques to bring characters and stories to life.

    SQFilma të animuar – filma të krijuar me vizatime, imazhe kompjuterike ose teknika të tjera animacioni.

  3. 03

    Crime Films

    ENFilms that focus on criminal activities, investigations, detectives, and the world of crime.

    SQFilma kriminalë – filma që përqendrohen te aktivitetet kriminale, hetimet, detektivët dhe bota e krimit.

    In this edition: Sherlock Holmes

  4. 04

    Documentaries

    ENFilms that present real people, events, places, or situations to inform or educate the audience.

    SQDokumentarë – filma që paraqesin njerëz, ngjarje, vende ose situata reale për të informuar ose edukuar publikun.

  5. 05

    Historical Dramas

    ENFilms based on historical events or periods, often portraying important people and events from the past.

    SQDrama historike – filma të bazuar në ngjarje ose periudha historike, që shpesh paraqesin figura dhe ngjarje të rëndësishme të së kaluarës.

    In this edition: The King’s Speech

  6. 06

    Horror Films

    ENFilms designed to frighten, shock, or create feelings of fear and suspense.

    SQFilma horror / tmerri – filma që synojnë të frikësojnë, të tronditin ose të krijojnë ndjenja frike dhe ankthi.

    In this edition: Let Me In

  7. 07

    Musicals

    ENFilms in which songs, music, and sometimes dancing are important parts of the story.

    SQMuzikalë – filma ku këngët, muzika dhe ndonjëherë vallëzimi janë pjesë të rëndësishme të historisë.

  8. 08

    Romantic Comedies

    ENFilms that combine love stories with humour and funny situations.

    SQKomedi romantike – filma që ndërthurin historitë e dashurisë me humorin dhe situatat komike.

    In this edition: The Proposal

  9. 09

    Science Fiction or Fantasy Films

    ENFilms that explore imaginary worlds, futuristic technology, space, magic, or supernatural elements.

    SQFilma fantastiko-shkencorë ose fantazi – filma që eksplorojnë botë imagjinare, teknologji futuriste, hapësirën, magjinë ose elemente mbinatyrore.

  10. 10

    Thrillers

    ENFilms that create excitement, tension, suspense, and uncertainty, keeping viewers on the edge of their seats.

    SQFilma thriller / triller – filma që krijojnë emocion, tension, ankth dhe pasiguri, duke e mbajtur publikun në ankth deri në fund.

Vocabulary in Use

Talking About Your Movie Preferences

Five ways to say how you feel about a film genre — from the strongest like to the strongest dislike.
ExpressionExample Sentence
I love…I love action and adventure films because they are exciting.
I really like…I really like animations because they have creative stories.
I quite like…I quite like historical dramas because I enjoy learning about the past.
I don’t really like…I don’t really like horror films because they are too scary.
I can’t stand…I can’t stand boring documentaries because they make me lose interest.
Strong likeStrong dislike
I love…I really like…I quite like…I don’t really like…I can’t stand…

How strong is each expression?

The five expressions go from the strongest positive feeling to the strongest negative one. I love… shows a very strong preference. I really like… is strongly positive. I quite like… is positive but moderate. I don’t really like… is a mild, polite way to show dislike. I can’t stand… expresses a very strong dislike.
